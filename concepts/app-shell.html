One place for the record actions. Creating, modifying and deleting an invoice now sit together next to Save, instead of being split between a global button and a menu. The cluster changes with what you are doing: viewing shows New, Modify and Delete; editing or creating swaps Modify for Save and Undo, so nothing on screen is meaningless. Modify puts the record into edit mode, Save takes it back out, Undo discards and returns it to the saved version. The rules follow the document: a posted invoice cannot be modified or deleted, a canceled one is kept in the records rather than removed, and deleting always asks first — with the difference between deleting and cancelling spelled out, since only one of them leaves a trace. The Layout block that was never offered by the product has been dropped from Print Settings across every version.

1 · Record Navigator. The layout we settled on in v8. The invoice number and its status sit at the top, the four menus keep their own row, and moving between records stays at the bottom next to the documents you can generate. Nobody has to relearn anything — the cost is two rows of height that the form could have used.
